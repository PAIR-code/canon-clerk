A `Rationale` directive MUST be omitted when the underlying engineering trade-off or failure mode is self-evident.

Rationale: Citing Strunk & White (*The Elements of Style*, Rule 17), vigorous writing is concise; because the consumer is a frontier reasoning model, reciting universal engineering maxims dilutes prompt attention.
