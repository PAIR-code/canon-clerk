Each canon MUST codify a falsifiable invariant.

Rationale: Evaluator models cannot reach deterministic, binary verdicts on unfalsifiable or purely aspirational assertions, producing arbitrary check failures and contributor friction.

**Guidance:** Reformulate the invariant into a concrete, testable assertion that defines an unambiguous condition of violation rather than an aspirational goal.
