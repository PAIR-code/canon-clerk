Every sentence within a canon MUST omit needless words, expressing its meaning with maximal brevity and zero rhetorical padding.

Rationale: Citing Strunk & White (*The Elements of Style*, Rule 17), a sentence should contain no unnecessary words for the same reason a machine has no unnecessary parts; because the consumer is a frontier AI model, conversational padding and redundant qualifiers dilute attention mechanisms without supplying new semantic signal.
