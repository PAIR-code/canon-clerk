Following ISO/IEC Directives Part 2 (Normative References) and RFC 2119 specification practice, wire formats, syntaxes, CLI conventions, configuration schemas, and protocols introduced in architecture specifications MUST align with and cite the governing industry standard or established precedent.

Exception: A specification MAY introduce a bespoke format or convention IFF it explicitly documents why existing industry standards cannot satisfy the stated architectural requirements.
