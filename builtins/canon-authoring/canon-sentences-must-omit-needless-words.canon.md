Every sentence within a canon MUST omit needless words, expressing its meaning with maximal brevity and zero rhetorical padding.

Precedent: Strunk & White (*The Elements of Style*, Rule 17).

Rationale: Because the consumer is a frontier AI model, conversational padding and redundant qualifiers dilute attention mechanisms without supplying new semantic signal.
