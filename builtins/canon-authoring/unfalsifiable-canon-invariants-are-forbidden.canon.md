Canon invariants, whether stated in the body or derived from the filename or heading when the body omits an invariant clause, MUST define testable conditions of violation; unfalsifiable or purely aspirational assertions are forbidden.

Rationale: Evaluator models cannot reach binary verdicts on aspirational goals, inducing review friction and non-deterministic check failures.
